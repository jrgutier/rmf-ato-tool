### Q2 {#INT-004.Q2}
Asked of: PER-CIO · Task: P-2
**A2 {#INT-004.A2}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[INT-001.A1]] [[ASM-004]]
> Risk tolerance, as AOs apply it: we rate risk on the SP 800-30 five-level scale. An AO may
> accept Very Low, Low and Moderate risk on their own. A system whose overall risk is High or
> Very High needs my documented concurrence before an ATO is issued, and Very High risk to
> individuals' privacy is not accepted at all without the senior official for privacy as well.
> The maximum authorization period is three years; a decision with conditions may be shorter.
> Weaknesses are remediated or carry a POA&M item with milestones: Very High within 15 days,
> High within 30 days, Moderate within 90 days, Low within 180 days of the finding. By type of
> loss we tolerate moderate risk to mission operations and organizational assets, and low risk
> to individuals, other organizations and the Nation.
