### Q3 {#INT-144.Q3}
Asked of: PER-CISO · Task: A-1 · Control focus: ca-2 (reuse of previous results)
What does the Component allow assessors to reuse from earlier tests and assessments?
**A3 {#INT-144.A3}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[ART-ORG-CONTEXT]]
> Our assessment and continuous monitoring procedure, POL-02, lets the SCA reuse a result no older
> than 365 days when the assessment starts, from an assessment at least as independent as the one
> required, on the same release or one whose changes do not touch the controls concerned. The SCA
> judges credibility and applicability; the AO or AODR approves the reuse. Developer and program
> self-test results the SCA did not witness are not reused as results: the SCA examines them as
> evidence and re-performs a sample. Providers' assessments under their own authorizations are
> leveraged after the SCA reviews them.
