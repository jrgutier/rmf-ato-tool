  - `--family sa` reported `0 error(s)`.
  - `--task A-3` reported `error ASMT-005 artifacts/sar/results/sa.yaml: FND-022 names SA-11b. as affected by the finding, but that statement is satisfied`.

- Workaround used: Set `SA-11b.` to other-than-satisfied (see F-13-3). The second cycle reported 0. Journal RETRY at 06:05Z.
- Recommendation: Make `--family <fam>` run every rule that reads `sar/results/<fam>.yaml` or `ssp/controls/<fam>.yaml`, ASMT-005 included. Add a fixture to show that `--family` and `--task` agree on a family-local error.

### F-13-2: The plan's additional procedures (APR-###) have nowhere to be recorded in the SAR
- Where: `sap.yaml` `additional_procedures` (APR-001 REQ-11, APR-002 REQ-12); `framework/schema/sar-results.schema.json` (determinations only by `control_id`); `assessment.py` (APR read only by ASMT-002 `_methods_fit`)
- Kind: gap
- Severity: minor
- What happened:
  - A-2 added two additional procedures with their own determination statements for requirements that no SP 800-53 control states.
  - A determination is keyed by `control_id`, and its `statements` must be the control's catalog objectives (ASMT-005). So APR-001 and APR-002 cannot have results of their own.
  - No rule checks that an APR was ever assessed. A SAR that ignores them passes.
- Workaround used: Stated the APR results in the sa-11 summary and in FND-022, and set `SA-11b.` other-than-satisfied: S-3 allocated REQ-11 and REQ-12 to sa-11 as developer verification, and SA-11b is the testing statement. This choice is an interpretation; an assessor could argue for SA-11a or for no control statement at all.
- Recommendation: Add `additional_results` to the family file, or a top-level `artifacts/sar/additional.yaml`, holding `{apr_id, statements: [{text, result}], finding_refs}`. Add a rule that every APR of the plan has a result after A-3.

### F-13-3: The line between a simulated re-performance and an invented pass is not defined for synthetic runs
- Where: A-3 in synthetic mode; `playbooks/_shared/interviewing.md`; `playbooks/A/A-3.md` step 4; the brief ("no invented passes or failures")
- Kind: gap
- Severity: major
- What happened:
  - Many plan procedures ask the assessor to test something the product describes only as a design. Examples: the ingest field filter (pt-2), the purge (si-12), key policy refusal (sc-12), the unencrypted-bucket refusal (sc-28), console session expiry (ma-4) and the re-tagged image (sr-3).
  - The product gives a related test result for some of these, such as admission signature verification "pass" and the DT-2 tamper case. For others it gives only the mechanism (privacy.md "purged monthly"), and for the field filter only an S-4 interview.
  - I recorded simulated passes where the product describes the mechanism and nothing contradicts it.
  - I recorded "not demonstrated" under an ASSUMPTION where the plan targets an open item and the product has no result: the PT-3 retest (ASM-022), REQ-11 and REQ-12 (ASM-023), and release 1.5.2 (ASM-024).
  - This is one reasonable line. The playbooks do not state it, and another agent could make all of these satisfied or all of them not-evaluated. Each choice changes R-2's risk picture.
- Workaround used: Applied the line above consistently. The ART-SAR assessment summary lists the five assumption-based results, and sar-summary.yaml lists ASM-020 to ASM-024 under `assumptions`.
- Recommendation: Add a "simulated evidence" rule to `_shared/interviewing.md` or A-3. A simulated test may reproduce only a result the product states, or a mechanism the product describes with no contrary evidence. A test aimed at an open item, or at something the product never mentions, gets `not demonstrated` under an ASSUMPTION. Better still, add a `basis: product | simulated | assumption` flag on statements (F-12-4), so that R-2 can weigh them.

### F-13-5: The penetration test results block is single-valued, while the plan schedules a second test that supplements a reused first one
- Where: `framework/schema/sar-summary.schema.json` `penetration_test` (one object, one `date`); `sap.yaml` `penetration_test` (2026-11-30) plus RR-002 (the first test, 2026-10-27 to 2026-10-31); PENT-002
- Kind: gap
- Severity: minor
- What happened:

  - The only exploitation the product records is from the first test, which the plan reuses (RR-002).

- Recommendation: Make `penetration_test` a list keyed by test (with `reused: RR-###` for a reused one), or add `supplements: RR-###` with separate results. Have PENT-002 require results for the planned test and cite the reused one.

### F-13-6: `sar-summary.yaml` asks for each person's name, but the run must not name real or invented people
- Where: `templates/data/sar-summary.yaml` `environment.personnel[].name`, `sca.name`, `sca.email`, `sca.phone`; the brief ("never name a real product, vendor, unit or person")
- Kind: friction
- Severity: note
- What happened:
  - CNSSI 1254 Annex C asks for the names of everyone present. The schema requires `name` for each person and an e-mail and phone for the SCA.
  - The product names roles only, and the stakeholders file gives no people.
- Workaround used: Used role titles as names ("SCA team lead (role title)"), an example.mil address and the phone 000-000-0000.
- Recommendation: Allow `role_title` instead of `name` in the schema, or say in the template that a synthetic run uses role titles. Add a `contact` placeholder convention.

### F-13-7: Statements inherited from a provider inside a hybrid control have no inherited marker
- Where: si-2 (`SI-02b.[03]`/`[04]` and `SI-02c.[02]`, firmware, which the IL4 provider patches); ir-4, si-4 and ra-5 (the CSSP parts); OCTX-006 (only `status: inherited` controls)
- Kind: gap
- Severity: minor
- What happened:
  - OCTX-006 and the `inherited` block apply only to a control whose SSP status is `inherited`. They are well built for that case: pe-3 and mp-6 passed on the first cycle.

- Recommendation: Allow a `basis: provider-assessment` (with `provider: EXT-###`) on individual statements, and let OCTX-006 check those statements the same way.
