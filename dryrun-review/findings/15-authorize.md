### F-15-1: The decision date is forced past the run's clock, and then ASMT-010 makes the run invent two months of POA&M progress
- Where: R-4 (`authorizations.yaml` date); PRIV-003 (`pia_approval`); MPOA-001; ASMT-010 `_latest()`; `run.yaml as_of`
- Kind: defect
- Severity: major
- What happened: Three rules pin the decision. PRIV-003 refuses an authorization dated before the PIA approval, which P-14 recorded for 2027-01-25 through an assumption (ASM-011). MPOA-001 refuses an authorization dated before the 17 before-authorization items scheduled up to 2027-01-25 (F-14-9). The R-4 playbook says only "on or after `run.yaml:as_of`" (2026-11-18). But once the ledger holds a decision dated 2027-01-25, ASMT-010 takes that date as "the latest date the run vouches for" and fails every POA&M milestone due before it that is neither `completed` nor changed. About 30 milestones were affected. So the run must state how two months it cannot see went. The other options are worse. A December decision on the package as submitted breaks PRIV-003 and MPOA-001, or needs A-6's recommendations rewritten. A decision on 2027-01-26 would add a day of missed milestones. Nothing in the framework tells the agent this. The handoff anticipated it.
- Workaround used: decision dated 2027-01-25, the PIA approval day, so milestones due that day are not late. R-3 recorded the progress to the decision under one stated rule, ASM-025: milestones were met as planned, except where the plan named an outside obstacle. The two named obstacles were the software factory's base image and the EMIS legal review, and both slipped again. No finding is closed without the SCA. The records the met milestones would change are left to M-1/M-2 and named in the journal. Journal NOTE at 06:49Z.
- Recommendation: Give a synthetic run a simulated clock (for example `run.yaml: simulated_now`, advanced by tasks or by interview dates). Have ASMT-010, MPOA-001 and PRIV-003 read it. Have R-4's playbook state the earliest date the run's own records allow. Better still, have `rmf status` print it ("decision not before 2027-01-25: PIA approval, POAM-0xx"). Say in R-3 how to record POA&M progress between the package and the decision.
