# P-1..P-7 · Organization-level Prepare (organizational risk context)

<!-- rmf:body authored -->

Tasks P-1 to P-7 are the organization-level Prepare tasks. The DoD Component CIO, the PAO
and the capability portfolio manager complete them for the whole organization, not for one
system (DoDI 8510.01 §3.2.a). In a run they build one artifact, **ART-ORG-CONTEXT**: P-1
writes it, and P-2 to P-7 each append their own section. Every later task that lists
ART-ORG-CONTEXT as an input reads it from there.

Each task has its own section below, with a CLI-generated header (`rmf playbooks sync`) and
the usual playbook sections as H3 headings. `rmf status` names the task and links its
section (`playbooks/P/P-org-context.md#task-p-1`).

## How these tasks run

1. **Check whether the organization's context is already given.** Most forks start from an
   organization that already has its roles, strategy and common controls. In that case
   `run.yaml` lists ART-ORG-CONTEXT under `seeded`, and the tasks are `deferred` with
   `kind: performed-externally` and a rationale; `rmf status` then skips them. Do the tasks
   here only when `rmf status` names them. Why: re-deriving an organization's strategy
   inside one system's run invents policy the system has to obey.
2. **Speak to organization-level personas.** The respondents are the CIO, CISO, risk
   executive, privacy officer, AO and the capability sponsor, not the program. Their cards
   say what each knows; the program office (PER-PM) is informed or consulted at most. Why:
   SP 800-37r2 assigns these tasks to the head of agency and senior officials.
3. **Ground answers in the organization's policy.** EXT-ORG-POLICY is organizational policy
   and Component Prepare-step guidance in the product directory. When a fork does not
   supply it, record what the persona states as the organization's position, and use
   assumptions (`_shared/tagging-and-traceability.md`) for what nobody can answer.
4. **One section per task.** ART-ORG-CONTEXT has one H2 section per task, in this order
   (`templates/artifacts/ART-ORG-CONTEXT.md`):

   | Task | Section |
   |---|---|
   | P-1 | `## RMF role assignments` |
   | P-2 | `## Risk management strategy and risk tolerance` |
   | P-3 | `## Organization-level risk assessment` |
   | P-4 | `## Organizationally tailored baselines and profiles` |
   | P-5 | `## Common controls available for inheritance` |
   | P-6 | `## Impact-level prioritization` |
   | P-7 | `## Organizational continuous monitoring strategy` |

   P-1 writes the file from the template, filling only `## RMF role assignments`, and keeps
   the template's HTML comment that lists what each later section holds. Each later task
   rewrites the whole file with its own section appended at the end, after the sections
   already there, and itself appended to `touched_by`. A task that is not performed in
   the organization (optional P-4, P-6) still appends its heading with one traced sentence
   saying the organization has none. Why: RUN-PH-001 forbids a placeholder left for a later
   task, TRACE-001 needs a token in every section, and the file is written whole (INV-8).
5. **Build, check and journal** after each task, as for any task (`00-run-protocol.md`,
   steps 7 and 8).

Purpose: Identify and assign the individuals who fill the security and privacy risk management roles, free of conflicts of interest, before system-level RMF work starts with the program.
<!-- END rmf:header P-1 -->

### Steps

1. **Entry check.** P-1 has no dependencies. EXT-ORG-POLICY (organizational charts,
   appointment letters, Component policy) is in the product directory, or the CIO can state
   the assignments.
2. **Read** the role definitions:
   `uv run rmf refs show DODI8510.01 --page 30 --anchor "Appointment of RMF Roles"` (Table 8:
   who appoints whom) and `uv run rmf refs show SP800-37R2 --section "TASK P-1"`.
3. **Interview** PER-CIO, then PER-PRIV (`### Interviews`): who fills each RMF role for the
   organization and for this system's portfolio (AO, AODR, CISO and SCA, risk executive,
   privacy officer, common control providers), who appointed them, and any role one person
   holds together with another.
4. **Check separation.** No AO is also the system owner or common control provider of what
   it authorizes, and the SCA has the independence the AO requires. A conflict is a
   finding to record, not to resolve here. Why: the exit criterion and SP 800-37r2 Task P-1
   require it.
5. **Write `artifacts/ART-ORG-CONTEXT.md`** from `templates/artifacts/ART-ORG-CONTEXT.md`
   with `produced_by_task: P-1` and `touched_by: [P-1]`. Fill `## RMF role assignments` with
   a table of role, persona ID, holder's title (not a personal name unless the fork gives
   one) and appointing authority, traced. Do not add the later sections; P-2 to P-7 append
   them (step 4 of "How these tasks run").
6. **Build and check.** `uv run rmf build <run>`, then `uv run rmf check <run> --task P-1`,
   until 0 errors. **Journal** a CHECKPOINT.

### Interviews

| Persona | Why | Question focus |
|---|---|---|
| PER-CIO | responsible and accountable (R, A) | role holders and appointing authorities, combined roles |
| PER-PRIV | responsible (R) | the senior privacy official and system privacy roles |
| PER-AO (optional) | consulted (C) | the AODRs appointed, the independence required of the SCA |

At least 1 interview with `task: P-1`.

### Inputs

- EXT-ORG-POLICY: organizational charts, appointment letters and policy in the product
  directory, cited as `product/<path>#<anchor>`

Purpose: Establish the organization's risk management strategy, including how it frames, assesses, responds to and monitors risk and the risk tolerance that the Component CIO communicates to programs.
<!-- END rmf:header P-2 -->

### Steps

1. **Entry check.** P-1 is done (ART-ORG-CONTEXT lists P-1).
2. **Read** `uv run rmf refs show SP800-39 --section "2.3.3"` (risk tolerance in the
   strategy) and `uv run rmf refs show SP800-37R2 --section "TASK P-2"`.
3. **Interview** PER-CIO and PER-RE: the organization's risk management strategy (how risk
   is framed, assessed, responded to and monitored), its **risk tolerance** stated so a
   later decision can be checked against it (for example which risk levels the AO may
   accept, and for how long), and the constraints and priorities that shape it. Ask
   PER-PRIV for the privacy part. Ask PER-RE how risk assessments are maintained: what
   triggers an update, how often, what is updated and to whom results are communicated.
4. **Rewrite `artifacts/ART-ORG-CONTEXT.md`** whole: append `P-2` to `touched_by` and append
   `## Risk management strategy and risk tolerance`, traced, including the risk assessment
   maintenance approach. S-5 records it for the system in `conmon.yaml#risk_assessment` and
   M-4 applies it. Keep the risk tolerance
   concrete; R-2 and R-4 compare the system's risk with it.
   Record also the Component CIO's process for designated approval authorities to make
   certification determinations and issue an IATT or ATO expeditiously for a capability fielded
   under urgent capability acquisition (DoDI 5000.81), so a UCA program knows whom to ask.
5. **Build and check** with `--task P-2`, then **journal** a CHECKPOINT.

| PER-PRIV (optional) | consulted (C) | privacy risk in the strategy |
