# Organization and program documents

What the organization and the acquisition program already decided. The Prepare tasks at the
organization level (risk strategy, common controls, threats, capabilities) and the
requirements tasks read this file; without it, those answers have to be simulated.

## Appointments

Who holds each RMF role (AO, AODR, CIO, CISO, SCA, risk executive, privacy officer, ISSM,
ISSO, user representative), who appointed them and by which letter or charter. An
organization chart is enough. Without it, P-1 records the roles as assumptions.

## Policy and risk strategy

The Component's RMF policy, risk tolerance, and any organization-wide risk management
strategy or continuous monitoring strategy the system must follow. Name the documents and
their versions.

If the organization's context is already settled (most forks), say so here: the run can
then seed ART-ORG-CONTEXT and defer P-1 to P-7 as `performed-externally`
(`playbooks/P/P-org-context.md`, step 1 of "How these tasks run").

## Common controls

Controls the organization or a hosting environment provides to every system (for example
physical protection, training, incident response), and who provides them.

## Threat information

Threat assessments that apply (for example a validated threat report or a cyber threat
summary), at the level this repository may hold.

## Capability and requirements documents

The capability document (or user agreement for a software program), key performance
parameters and any cybersecurity survivability requirement.

## Acquisition documents

Program protection plan (PPP), cybersecurity strategy, systems engineering plan (SEP), test
and evaluation master plan or strategy, and registry records (for example DITPR or eMASS),
with versions and approval dates.

## Release history

Each release or increment fielded so far, its date and what changed, and the next planned
release.
