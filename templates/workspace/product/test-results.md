# Test results

Results the assessor may reuse: developer tests, developmental test events, configuration
and vulnerability scans, and operational records (for example account reviews). For each,
give the date, scope, method and result, under its own heading with an anchor.

## Test event or scan name

Date, scope (which units and components), what was tested, and the results, including
failures and open items.

## Configuration compliance

For each STIG or SRG checked: the benchmark and its version, the date, the tool, the number
of open findings by category (CAT I, II, III), and the rule IDs of any open finding with its
planned fix or accepted deviation.
