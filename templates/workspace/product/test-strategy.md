# Test strategy

The TEMP or test strategy the pathway calls for, as the program has it. A-2 reads this into
`artifacts/data/test-strategy.yaml`; anything missing here becomes a simulated answer.

## Document

The document's title, version and date, who approved it (the Component; DOT&E or USD(R&E)
for a program on oversight), and whether the program is on the DOT&E oversight list.

## Cybersecurity test objectives

The objectives, the entry and exit criteria of each phase, and the evaluation frameworks.

## Test events

For each planned or held DT and OT event: its kind (cooperative vulnerability
identification, CVPA, adversarial assessment, penetration test, DCO evaluation), dates, the
conducting organization, whether the testers are independent and threat representative,
whether the CSSP defends, the decision point it supports, and whether the system under test
is production representative (build, environment, configuration).
