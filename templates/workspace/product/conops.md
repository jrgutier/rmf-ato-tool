# Concept of operations

Replace each section's guidance with your system's description. Keep the headings: run
artifacts cite them by slug (`product/conops.md#known-limitations`).

## Mission

What mission or business function the system supports, what it does and does not do, and
what happens to the mission when it fails.

## Program and lifecycle

The owning program office, acquisition pathway and phase, developer or integrator, number
of units or sites now and planned, and the current lifecycle state (operational, under
development, under major modification).

## Operational mode summary and mission profile

How the system is used over time: its operating modes, the tasks done in each, and how long and how often (OMS/MP), when the capability document gives none.

## Users

A table of user groups: count, kind of access (none, non-privileged, privileged; remote or
local) and what they do. Include people who only see the system's outputs elsewhere.

## Operating environment

Where the system runs, its physical setting, the networks and enclaves it sits in, and the
supporting services it relies on.

## Information handled

Each kind of information the system processes, stores or transmits, what its disclosure,
tampering or loss would cause, and whether it includes PII or classified information.

## Availability need

How long the mission can tolerate an outage and the program's recovery goal.

## Known limitations

Open engineering items and known security gaps the program has already identified, with the
release or date that fixes each. Disclose them here: the assessor and the AO will find them
anyway, and hiding them makes the package wrong.
