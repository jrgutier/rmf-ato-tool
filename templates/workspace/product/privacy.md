# Privacy

Fill this in when the system collects, stores, uses or shares personally identifiable
information (PII). Leave a single line saying "The system handles no PII" otherwise. The
categorization, the privacy impact assessment and the privacy controls cite it.

## PII handled

Which PII, about whom (service members, civilians, contractors, the public), roughly how many
records, and which information types carry it.

## Authority and notice

The legal authority for collecting it, the system of records notice (SORN) if one applies,
and the Privacy Act statement shown to individuals.

## Privacy impact assessment

The PIA's status (draft, in review, approved), its version and date, who reviews and approves
it, and any open questions from the privacy office.

## Minimization and retention

How collection is kept to what is needed, the records schedule, and how records are disposed.
