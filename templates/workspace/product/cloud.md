# Cloud service offerings

Only for a system hosted on a cloud service offering (CSO). One section per offering. Each
answer here becomes a traced source for `artifacts/data/cloud.yaml` (DTM 24-001), so record
what the documents say, not what a person remembers.

## Offering name

- **Provisional authorization:** the PA letter's issuer, date, impact level, path (FedRAMP
  or DoD) and conditions, and where the package sits in eMASS.
- **Service model:** IaaS, PaaS or SaaS, per part of the offering the system uses.
- **Customer responsibility matrix:** the controls the offering implements, those it shares
  with the tenant, and those left to the tenant.
- **Contract:** the cybersecurity clauses (DFARS 252.239-7010 and the others DTM 24-001
  lists), the incident reporting and the recipient of the defensive cyber operations data.
- **Activity tracking checklist:** for each DTM 24-001 Table 1 activity, who performs it
  (the CSP, the CSSP, the mission owner, a DoD entity).
- **CSSP:** its DoD O-8530.01-M evaluation (date, authorizing body), its duties for this
  offering, its visibility into the tenant, and the last test of its detection.
- **External assessments:** red team, penetration tests and CCRI coordination, with the
  written approvals and the dates mission details were sent.
- **User activity monitoring:** who performs it and how it is correlated with the insider
  threat program.
