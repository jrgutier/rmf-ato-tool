Asked of: PER-PRIV · Task: S-4 · Control focus: pt-1, pt-2, pt-3, pt-4

**A1 {#INT-050.A1}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[INT-015.A1]] [[product/conops.md#information]]
> Privacy policy comes from the Component privacy program. The station processes only the names
