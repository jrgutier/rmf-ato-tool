### Q2 {#INT-039.Q2}
Asked of: PER-ISSM · Task: S-4 · Control focus: at-3, at-3.1, at-3.2, at-3.5, at-4, at-6
What role-based training is given, and how is it recorded?

**A2 {#INT-039.A2}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[product/architecture.md#physical]] [[product/architecture.md#updates]]
> Operators and commanders get the program's operator course, which includes the tamper-seal
> check at setup and handling of removable media; maintainers get the maintainer course on
> update bundles and signature checks; S6 administrators and the ISSO hold DoD cyber workforce
> qualifications. The station has no environmental control equipment of its own; it runs on

> keeps training records for five years, and the ISSO checks them at the quarterly account
> review; course feedback goes to the program office after each new-equipment training event.
