# INT-049 — S-4 — PER-ISSO

### Q1 {#INT-049.Q1}
Asked of: PER-ISSO · Task: S-4 · Control focus: ps-1, ps-2, ps-3, ps-3.4, ps-9
How are positions designated and people screened before they get access to a station?

**A1 {#INT-049.A1}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[product/conops.md#users]] [[ART-ORG-CONTEXT]]
> Personnel security policy comes from the Component program. Each station role (operator,
> mission commander, field maintainer, S6 administrator) carries a position designation in the
> unit's duty descriptions, and the privileged roles, maintainer and administrator, carry the
> higher designation. Before I approve a directory group or add someone to the maintainer list,
> the unit security manager confirms the person's investigation and eligibility meet the
> position designation, including the citizenship requirement. Re-screening follows the
> Component's continuous vetting.

### Q2 {#INT-049.Q2}
Asked of: PER-ISSO · Task: S-4 · Control focus: ps-4, ps-4.1, ps-5, ps-6, ps-6.3, ps-8
What happens when someone leaves or moves, and what do they sign?

**A2 {#INT-049.A2}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[product/conops.md#limitations]] [[INT-047.A1]]
> When a user leaves the unit, S6 disables the directory account and I remove the person from
> the maintainer list the same day; the platoon changes the shared gcsmaint password when a
> maintainer leaves, and the person turns in any station media. The unit security manager's
> out-processing briefing covers the duty to protect mission information after leaving. A
> transfer inside the brigade is handled the same way: the old group is removed and a new one
> approved for the new role. Everyone signs the station rules of behavior and the Component
> nondisclosure agreement before access, and violations go to the commander under the
> Component's sanctions process, with the ISSM notified.

### Q3 {#INT-049.Q3}
Asked of: PER-ISSO · Task: S-4 · Control focus: ps-7
What personnel security applies to the integrator's people?

**A3 {#INT-049.A3}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[product/conops.md#top]]
> The integrator's contract carries the same screening requirements as the station roles and
> requires the integrator to tell the program office within one working day when one of its
> people with test-station access leaves or changes role. Integrator staff work only on the
> pre-production test stations; the program office monitors compliance at each contract review.
