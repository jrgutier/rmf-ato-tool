Asked of: PER-RE · Task: P-2
**A2 {#INT-002.A2}** respondent: simulated · tag: SIMULATED · basis: [[ASM-002]]
> Our tolerance is stated in levels. An AO may accept residual risk rated low or moderate. A
> residual risk rated high may be accepted only with Component CIO concurrence before the ATO

> not accepted; the system does not operate until it is reduced.
