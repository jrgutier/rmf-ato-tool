# ETGCS monitoring records, cycle 2026-10 {#top}

> **FICTIONAL — FOR DEMONSTRATION.** No real system, scan or review is described.

Operational monitoring records the program hands to the RMF team for the first monitoring
cycle (EXT-MONITORING). They cover the six pre-production stations at the fictional test
range, 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-28. Each record says who produced it, its scope and its result.

## Configuration compliance checks {#config-checks}

- Producer: the stations' automated start-up and pre-upload compliance check, summarized by
  the ETGCS ISSO (2026-10-28).
- Scope: all six stations, 41 start-ups and 37 mission uploads.
- Result: no drift from the approved baseline on the workstation image or the router. One
  pre-upload check on `etgcs-p3-ws` (2026-10-14) flagged a changed screen-lock setting
  after a maintainer session under `gcsmaint`; the check blocked the upload, the setting
  was restored, and the event could not be tied to a person (see `#account-review`).
- The mission planner (RouteCraft Planner 7) is outside the automated check, because no
  STIG or benchmark exists for it; its hand-applied settings were not re-verified.

## STIG scan after the 2026-10 bundle {#stig-scan}

- Producer: SCA-witnessed scan run by the integrator (2026-10-24), after the 2026-10 signed
  update bundle was installed on all six stations (2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23).
- Scope: workstation image (Windows STIG) and router (network device STIG) on six stations.
- Result: baseline in place with the deviations already in the STIG summary; no new open
  findings. The mission planner was not scanned (no STIG).

## Vulnerability scan after the 2026-10 bundle {#vuln-scan}

- Producer: integrator scan (2026-10-24).
- Workstation and router: the 3 medium findings reported on 2026-09-15 are fixed by the
  2026-10 bundle; no new high or critical findings.
- Ground data terminal: the management web service vulnerability is still open on all six
  stations (firmware 5.1.8). Firmware 5.2.1 is in regression testing for the 2026-12 bundle;
  the management service is still reachable only from the station internal network.
- Mission planner: the 2 medium findings in the bundled library are still open; the vendor
  has given no fix date.

## Weekly log reviews {#log-reviews}

- Producer: ETGCS ISSO weekly station report (four weeks, 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-26).
- Workstation and router logs reached the enclave collector after each reconnect (the
  longest disconnected period was 3 days, during an exercise). The brigade S6 reported no
  anomalies in the forwarded logs.
- Mission planner and GDT logs are not forwarded; the ISSO reviewed them by hand on each
  station during the weekly review. No anomalies.
- One burst of 6 failed CAC sign-ins on `etgcs-p2-ws` (2026-10-17) was a card-reader fault,
  confirmed with the operator; the account lockout worked as configured.

## Account review {#account-review}

- Producer: ETGCS ISSO quarterly account review (2026-10-27).
- 14 CAC accounts, all approved in the brigade directory groups; 6 sealed break-glass
  accounts, none used; the shared `gcsmaint` account on each workstation and router, used by
  5 maintainers, unchanged. The GDT vendor administrator passwords were confirmed unique per
  station.
- Release 2.4 (per-maintainer local accounts with offline CAC sign-in) is still planned for
  2027-02.

## Incidents and events {#incidents}

- No security incidents were reported to the CSSP during the cycle.
- No physical tamper-seal exceptions at the 22 kit setups.
